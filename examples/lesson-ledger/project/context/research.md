# Research: independent music teachers (example, desk research)

This file is an example fixture. Quotes are illustrative, not real sources.

## Target user
Independent music teachers giving private lessons (piano, guitar, violin) to 5-30 students, usually paid per lesson or monthly, in cash or by bank transfer.

## Problem, in their words
- "I keep my lessons in a notebook, and at the end of the month I don't know who has paid me."
- "I feel awkward chasing a payment when I'm not even sure the student owes it."
- "Music school software is expensive and complicated for someone who works alone."

## Current alternatives
| Alternative | Price | Why it falls short |
|---|---|---|
| Paper notebook | Free | No totals, easy to lose track of who owes what |
| Spreadsheet | Free | Tedious to keep up to date, no clear view of pending payments |
| Music school management software | $20-60/month | Built for schools, too complex for one teacher |

## Gap
A tool that does only three things for a single teacher: record a lesson, see what is pending, mark it paid.

## Strongest argument against
Many teachers are comfortable with a notebook and may not see enough value to pay.
