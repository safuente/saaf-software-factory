# Demo script: Lesson Ledger (English US, voice: Piper en_US-lessac-medium)

Total duration: 50.2 s

## Scene 1: hook (0.0-5.9 s)
- Narration (0.3-4.9 s): I keep my lessons in a notebook, and at the end of the month I don't know who has paid me.
- Captions: I keep my lessons in a notebook, | and at the end of the month | I don't know who has paid me.
- Actions: Title card: "Independent music teachers say" plus the research quote, revealed word by word as it is read.
- Expected screen: Quote on screen is read verbatim by the narration.
- Last screen change at narration end.

## Scene 2: login (5.9-12.8 s)
- Narration (6.2-11.8 s): Log in with your email and password to open your ledger. Your lessons and payments are all in one place.
- Captions: Log in with your email and password to open your ledger. | Your lessons and payments are all in one place.
- Actions: Type teacher@example.com and the password (masked), click Log in as the narration ends.
- Expected screen: Login form, then the summary.
- Last screen change at narration end.

## Scene 3: summary (12.8-21.1 s)
- Narration (13.1-20.1 s): Your monthly summary shows lessons given, money collected, and money still pending, so you always know where you stand.
- Captions: Your monthly summary shows lessons given, money collected, | and money still pending, | so you always know where you stand.
- Actions: Highlight Lessons given, Collected, then Pending.
- Expected screen: November summary: 5 lessons, $85 collected, $60 pending.
- Last screen change at narration end.

## Scene 4: record (21.1-28.6 s)
- Narration (21.4-27.6 s): After each lesson, record the student, the date, how long it ran, and the price. It takes seconds.
- Captions: After each lesson, record the student, the date, | how long it ran, and the price. | It takes seconds.
- Actions: Open Record lesson; choose Liam Brooks (guitar), date Nov 13, 45 min, $25; click Save lesson as the narration ends.
- Expected screen: Form, then summary with 6 lessons.
- Last screen change at narration end.

## Scene 5: pending (28.6-35.5 s)
- Narration (28.9-34.5 s): The pending payments list shows every lesson that is still unpaid. When a student pays, mark it as paid.
- Captions: The pending payments list shows every lesson that is still unpaid. | When a student pays, mark it as paid.
- Actions: Open Pending payments; click Mark as paid on Sofia Reyes as the narration ends.
- Expected screen: Pending list with 3 rows, then 2.
- Last screen change at narration end.

## Scene 6: outcome (35.5-42.6 s)
- Narration (35.8-41.6 s): Back on the summary, pending money has dropped and collected money has grown. No more guessing who owes you.
- Captions: Back on the summary, pending money has dropped | and collected money has grown. | No more guessing who owes you.
- Actions: Open Summary; highlight Pending, Collected and the lesson table.
- Expected screen: 6 lessons, $115 collected, $55 pending.
- Last screen change at narration end.

## Scene 7: close (42.6-50.2 s)
- Narration (42.9-48.7 s): Lesson Ledger. Record every lesson and see what is pending. Know who has paid you, without the notebook.
- Captions: Lesson Ledger. | Record every lesson and see what is pending. | Know who has paid you, without the notebook.
- Actions: Closing card: Lesson Ledger; tagline revealed word by word.
- Expected screen: Product name and value proposition.
- Last screen change at narration end.
